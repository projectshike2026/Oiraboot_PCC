ABOUT THE UNIVERSITY:
Oiraboot Academic University is an accredited private university established in Chattogram, Bangladesh. The university operates under the Private University Act 2010 and is approved by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and the Ministry of Education.

ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS:
- Minimum GPA 2.50 in both SSC (Secondary School Certificate) and HSC (Higher Secondary Certificate) or equivalent O/A Levels.
- Alternatively, a combined minimum GPA of 6.00 in SSC and HSC together, with no individual GPA below 2.00.
- Engineering Programs (CSE, EEE, Civil Engineering, Textile Engineering): Candidates must have completed HSC from Science background with Mathematics and Physics.
- General Programs (BBA, English, LL.B.): Open to candidates from Science, Business Studies, or Humanities backgrounds.

ADMISSION STEPS:
Step 1: Collect the Admission Application Form from the Admission Office or complete the online application portal (Application Form Fee: BDT 500).
Step 2: Submit certified copies of SSC and HSC marksheets, academic certificates, 4 passport-size photographs, and photocopy of NID or Birth Certificate.
Step 3: Appear for the brief Admission Assessment and Document Verification Viva at the South Khulshi Campus.
Step 4: Deposit the Admission Fee plus the First Trimester Tuition fee at the university accounts desk or nominated bank booths to receive your official Student ID and enrollment pack.

TUITION WAIVERS & SCHOLARSHIPS:
- Merit Waiver: Students achieving Golden GPA 5.00 in both SSC and HSC are eligible for up to 100% initial tuition fee waiver.
- Trimester GPA Waiver: Students maintaining CGPA 3.85 or above receive continuous merit waivers (20% to 100%).
- Female Student Waiver: Special 10% to 20% tuition concession for female applicants in engineering programs.
- Sibling / Spouse Waiver: 20% tuition fee discount for the second sibling or spouse studying concurrently.
- Freedom Fighter & Tribal Quotas: Full tuition fee waivers available in accordance with UGC guidelines.
