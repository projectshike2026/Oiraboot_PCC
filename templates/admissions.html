GPA

Standard Thresholds

Minimum GPA 2.50 in both SSC and HSC (or equivalent O/A Levels). Alternatively, combined minimum GPA of 6.00 with no individual score below 2.00.

SCI

Engineering Prerequisites

For B.Sc. in CSE, EEE, Civil, and Textile Engineering, candidates must have completed HSC from Science background with Mathematics and Physics.

GEN

General Programs & Fees

BBA, B.A. in English, and LL.B. (Hons) are open to candidates from Science, Business Studies, or Humanities backgrounds. Application form fee is BDT 500.
